📖 How to Play Faker

🎭

1. Roles

Citizens receive the **Secret Word**. The **Faker** receives only the category name!

💡

2. Submit Clues

Everyone submits a subtle 1-word clue. Citizens prove they know the word; the Faker blends in!

🗳️

3. Vote the Imposter

Discuss the clues and tap a player to secretly cast your vote on who gave a fake clue.

🏆

4. Scoring & Stealing

  • Faker escapes undetected: **Faker gets +2 pts**.
  • Faker caught: **Citizens get +1 pt**.
  • *Bonus*: Caught Faker can guess the secret word from options to **steal +2 pts**!
